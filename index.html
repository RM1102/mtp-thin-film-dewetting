M.Tech thesis (CLD 880) · Department of Chemical Engineering · IIT Delhi

A physics-informed neural network for spinodal dewetting of nanometre-thin liquid films

Rahul Arvind Masand · Supervisor: Prof. Jayati Sarkar · Results pages that accompany the report of 17 November 2026

What these pages show

A liquid film a few nanometres thick on a solid surface thins where it is thinnest and breaks up into drops by itself (spinodal dewetting). The thesis builds numerical solvers and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for this process, first with attraction only and then with Born repulsion, which stops the film at a thin floor. The pages below play the solver results as animations, so the break-up can be watched in the browser.
